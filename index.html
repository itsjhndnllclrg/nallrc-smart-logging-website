Why the new system?

Designed for students, not for paperwork.

📲

QR & NFC ID Scanning

Tap your student ID or scan the QR code. All your details are pulled instantly from the university database — no typing required.

⚡

Under 30-Second Log-in

From approach to entry in seconds. The only thing you choose is your purpose. Everything else is already filled in.

📊

Live Occupancy Data

See real-time library occupancy before you even leave your dorm. Plan your visit around peak hours to avoid waiting in line.

🔗

University SSO Integration

One account for everything — your existing student credentials work here. No new passwords, no new registrations.

💻

Web & Mobile Access

Log your visit from your phone before you arrive. Available on any device through the university portal or this web page.

🚪

Automated Exit Logging

Tap your ID on exit just as you did on entry. Accurate occupancy data, every time — for your benefit and the library's.
